What it does

One profile. Filled in, drafted, and tracked — with you always in control.

GradFill AI helps graduate and job applicants spend less time re-entering the same information across application portals. Nothing is ever submitted without you.

✓

Autofill from one profile

Save your education, experience, skills, contact details and preferences once. GradFill reuses them across application pages instead of you retyping the same answers every time.

?

Flags what it isn't sure about

Fields GradFill can't confidently fill are marked for your attention rather than guessed at or left silently blank — you always see what needs a second look before you submit.

AI

AI-drafted written answers

Ask for a draft of an open-ended application question and review it inside GradFill first. Edit it, save it to your answer library, or insert it — nothing is written or submitted without you choosing to.

AI

Resume Studio

Generates a role-specific resume draft using only the facts already saved in your profile — reordered and emphasised for the job description, never invented. Save multiple versions per role.

✓

Job Tracker

Saved → Applying → Applied → Assessment → Interview → Offer. Keep company, role, notes, links, the job-description snapshot, and which resume version you used, all in one place.

✓

You press Submit — not GradFill

GradFill fills and drafts. It never automates the final submission of an application. You review every field and every draft before it goes anywhere.

Pricing

Autofill and the tracker are free. AI drafting is the paid part.

The rules-based autofill engine, the Job Tracker, and your saved profile are free with no plan limit — they cost nothing to run and are never gated. Only the generative AI features (written-answer drafts, resume tailoring, cover letters) are metered and require Pro or a Season Pass.
