00A Application essentials

These are the details that repeatedly appear on Workday, PageUp and SuccessFactors forms. Confirm them here once so GradFill can complete picklists and repeating sections without stopping to ask you.

Work experience — business type

“Type of business” means the employer's industry or sector — for example Information Technology, Banking, Engineering, Energy or Retail. It is stored separately for each role; GradFill will only treat it as confirmed when you enter it yourself.

Education classification

Each qualification keeps its own broad education type, institution type and country. This lets GradFill answer fields such as Academic / University / Australia without guessing from another record.

Language proficiency

Confirm the language plus speaking, writing and reading levels used by application picklists. Non-English languages are prioritised when a form opens a Language Skills row.

07 Applications

08 Background

Grad programs love "select all that apply" checklists — programs you've done, leadership experience, extracurriculars, industries you've worked in. Tick what applies once here, and GradFill matches these directly against whatever a form asks, with no guessing involved. Worth being thorough: this is what makes those questions fill correctly instead of getting flagged for you every time.

09 Your examples
