GradFill AI

Privacy summary

This page describes the behaviour of the extension package. A public privacy-policy URL is still required before Chrome Web Store publication.

Local mode

Your profile, documents, tracker, and usage counters are stored in chrome.storage.local. No account or AI data is transmitted in Offline demo mode.

Cloud mode

Cloud sync is opt-in. When enabled, profile and tracker data are sent over HTTPS to the configured GradFill backend. The built-in sync intentionally strips the base64 contents of saved resume and transcript files; only their filename/type metadata is included in profile sync.

AI assistance

When you explicitly request an AI draft or tailored resume, GradFill sends the application question or job description together with relevant education, work experience, skills, languages, preferences and STAR examples. Demographic and accessibility fields are excluded from the default AI payload. AI output is a draft only and must be reviewed by the user.

Billing

The extension does not collect card details. Upgrade and billing buttons open a hosted payment page supplied by the GradFill backend/payment processor.

Submission

GradFill does not press a job application's final Submit button. The user reviews and submits the application.

Production checklist

Before public release, replace the placeholder API domain with your deployed backend, publish a full privacy policy and terms, configure your Chrome Web Store data disclosures, and make the disclosures consistent with the actual server/database retention practices.
