Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-18. This page describes exactly how the GradFill AI browser extension and its backend handle your data.

Local mode (default)

Your profile, saved documents, job tracker, and usage counters are stored in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device. In Offline demo mode, no account or AI data is transmitted anywhere.

Cloud sync (opt-in)

Cloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your profile and job tracker are sent over HTTPS to the GradFill backend so they can follow you across devices. Sync deliberately strips the actual file contents of a saved resume or transcript before sending — only the filename and file type are included, never the document itself.

AI-assisted drafting

AI features (drafted written answers, resume tailoring, cover letters) only run when you explicitly ask for them. When you do, GradFill sends the relevant application question or job description together with the career-relevant parts of your profile — education, work experience, skills, languages, preferences, and any STAR examples you've saved — to the GradFill backend, which forwards it to the configured AI provider. Demographic and accessibility fields are excluded from this payload by default. Every AI draft is shown to you inside GradFill for review before you can edit, save, or insert it — it is never generated or submitted silently.

Billing

GradFill does not collect or store your card details. Upgrade and billing actions open a hosted checkout/billing page run by our payment processor; GradFill's own backend never sees your payment card number.

What GradFill never does

GradFill does not press an application's final Submit button on your behalf — you always review and submit the application yourself. Referee/reference contact details are never auto-filled; GradFill flags those fields for you to complete manually. GradFill does not sell your data to third parties.

Your choices

You can use GradFill entirely offline in Offline demo mode, turn cloud sync on or off at any time from Setup, and delete your saved profile, documents, and tracker data from the extension at any time. Deleting your account (if you've created one) removes the corresponding data from the GradFill backend.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the support contact listed on GradFill AI's Chrome Web Store listing once it is published.
